Force.com Sites & Digital Experiences

We design and configure Salesforce-powered sites that extend your CRM to customers, partners, and internal users.

  • • Branded public or authenticated sites
  • • Secure access and role-based permissions
  • • Optimized user journeys for leads, support, and self-service

Third-Party Integrations

We connect Salesforce with the tools you already use so data moves reliably and in real time.

  • • REST and SOAP API integrations
  • • Webhook listeners and event-driven flows
  • • Real-time and scheduled data sync

Examples: payment gateways, email and SMS platforms, document storage, finance tools, and custom line-of-business systems.

Data Migration

We treat your data as an asset and move it into Salesforce with structure and control.

  • • Audit and cleanup of source data
  • • Template design and mapping for imports
  • • Use of tools such as Data Loader, Dataloader.io, and Import Wizard
  • • Pre- and post-migration scripts to validate and enrich data

Lightning & Custom Development

We build Lightning components and tailored solutions to match your business processes.

  • • Custom Lightning components and pages
  • • Enhancements to standard Salesforce actions
  • • Guided wizards for sales, service, and internal workflows
  • • Apex triggers, batch jobs, and scheduled automation with test coverage

Salesforce Configuration & Automation

We start with configuration before code, so your org stays maintainable and scalable.

  • • Object and field design, page layouts, and record types
  • • Web-to-Lead, Email-to-Case, and digital channels
  • • Automations with Flows, approval processes, and assignment rules
  • • Best-practice use of Apex only where configuration is not enough
